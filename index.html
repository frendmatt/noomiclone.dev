Swing on bars, flip off springboards and dive 50 m off cliffs into the pool. Physics works out every swing, slip and splash, so no two runs are the same.

The game

Swing, flip, dive

Hold a shape, add a twist and let go at the right moment. Noomi's ten-part ragdoll does the rest.

Map Maker

Build your own maps

Place bars, blocks, water, trampolines and diving boards, make pieces spin or move, and test the map on the spot. Then share it: anyone with its code can play it.

Every shared map gets a code and a link:noomiclone.dev/m/K7Q2-MBX

About

Made by Matt

Hi, I'm Matt. I grew up doing parkour and playing video games, and NoomiClone is where the two meet. My friend James West and I started the game together in 2020, and I took over full development in 2022.

A lot of it comes from our Discord: about 12,000 players who share runs, suggest maps and moves, and help me decide what to build next. Come and say hi.
